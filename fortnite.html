Fortnite Introduction

What is Fortnite

"Fortnite is a vibrant and strategic Battle Royale experience where 100 players drop onto an ever-changing island to scavenge for resources, build fortifications, and outlast rivals. By mastering both combat and rapid construction, players must stay ahead of a closing storm to be the last one standing and secure a Victory Royale."

Gameplay style

"Fortnite is a creative, high-energy third-person shooter that blends sharp gunplay with strategic building, where players must master resource gathering and rapid construction to outmaneuver opponents and survive the ever-shifting battlefield."
