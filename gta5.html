GTA V Introduction

What is GTA V

Grand Theft Auto V is a massive open-world action game where you play as three different criminals navigating the dangerous streets of Los Santos. Players can complete high-stakes heists, explore a vast world, and engage in a deep story or join millions of others in GTA Online. It is famous for its freedom, allowing you to drive, fly, and explore every corner of the map.

Gameplay style

GTA V offers a versatile open-world experience that blends third-person action, driving, and tactical missions, where players can switch between three unique characters to dominate the streets and execute complex heists.
