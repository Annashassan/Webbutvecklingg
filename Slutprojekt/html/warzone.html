Warzone Introduction

What is Warzone

"Call of Duty: Warzone is an intense, free-to-play Battle Royale experience where up to 150 players drop into a massive combat zone to scavenge for gear, eliminate rivals, and outrun a deadly gas cloud to be the last squad standing."

Gameplay style

Warzone is a fast-paced, high-octane first-person shooter that demands quick reflexes and strategic thinking, where players must master a wide array of weapons and vehicles to survive the chaos of the battlefield.
